## Contract Paraphrase

D1 demands that empirical conclusions survive challenges to how observations were obtained, processed, and analyzed. Reported uncertainty and information needed to repeat the work must make it possible to distinguish a dependable result from an artifact of analytical choices.

D2 requires technical assertions to withstand comparison with established knowledge. A skeptical assessment must detect misleading accounts of earlier research, misused specialist concepts, and factual mistakes that alter the interpretation of a result.

D3 requires the main proposition to follow from the premises and evidence actually supplied. Contradictions, unjustified inferential steps, and persuasive language that substitutes for support must not carry the reasoning on which the conclusion depends.

D4 requires readers from neighboring specialties to understand the intended scope and significance without supplying unstated assumptions. Claims that extend across specialties must earn that extension through adequate explanation and support.

D5 concerns whether presentation enables critical scrutiny: the sequence of ideas, precision of prose, and legibility of displays must expose the reasoning clearly. Applicable publication conventions must be respected without allowing polished presentation to conceal substantive ambiguity.

D6 requires an identifiable advance over existing knowledge whose importance is defensible for the intended publication audience. Any assessment of suitability must have an established target against which to challenge scope and contribution; no such alignment is asserted here.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Trace the central proposition through its stated assumptions, supporting observations, intermediate inferences, qualifications, and conclusion; challenge whether alternative explanations or changes of scope break that chain.
what_triggers_block: A material but repairable gap in the reasoning leaves a major claim unsupported, requiring substantial qualification, additional support, or reorganization before the existing central proposition can be assessed as established.
what_triggers_warn: A localized ambiguity, omitted qualification, or minor unsupported inference needs correction while the main chain of reasoning and its substantive conclusion remain supported.
what_triggers_fatal: A demonstrated contradiction or decisive counterexample defeats an indispensable premise or the central proposition itself, such that repairing the argument would require abandoning or replacing that proposition.
