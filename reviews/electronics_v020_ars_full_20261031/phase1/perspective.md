## Contract Paraphrase

D1 requires a research process that readers from neighboring disciplines can audit: the design, treatment of observations, uncertainty reporting, and available replication information must justify confidence in the findings under the relevant specialist standards.

D2 requires faithful use of the subject's concepts and evidence, so that readers bringing different disciplinary backgrounds are not misled by inaccurate terminology, distorted accounts of earlier studies, or incorrect substantive assertions.

D3 requires a consistent route from premises and observations to the main conclusion, with assumptions and inferential steps understandable across disciplinary boundaries and without reasoning defects that compromise the central case.

D4 requires sufficient explanation of motivation, vocabulary, and consequences for readers outside the immediate specialty, together with evidence and explicit limits for any asserted connections to other fields.

D5 requires an arrangement and presentation that help a mixed readership navigate the work, understand the prose and visual material, and recognize the publication format's expected elements.

D6 requires an identifiable contribution whose originality and importance are appropriate for the intended publication's audience, including an intelligible account of why that audience should care; assessing that requirement would need the relevant publication context.

## Scoring Plan

### D4: cross_disciplinary_relevance
dimension_id: D4
what_to_look_for: Definitions and framing that permit adjacent-field readers to follow the central contribution, explicit connections between disciplinary concepts, and evidence that supports the stated scope and practical or theoretical implications of those connections.
what_triggers_block: An essential conceptual bridge remains unexplained to adjacent-field readers, or a central interdisciplinary implication exceeds the evidence so substantially that evaluating its claimed relevance requires substantive revision.
what_triggers_warn: The central contribution and its scope are understandable, but localized gaps in definitions, contextual explanation, or qualification of secondary implications hinder adjacent-field readers and can be repaired without changing the central argument.
