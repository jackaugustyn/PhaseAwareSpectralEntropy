## Contract Paraphrase

D1 concerns whether the research process can withstand technical scrutiny. Editorial oversight depends on the methods specialist establishing that the design, treatment of observations, uncertainty reporting, and materials needed for independent checking support a dependable study.

D2 concerns the reliability of the manuscript's account of its research area. The editor needs the domain specialist to establish that technical statements are accurate, earlier contributions are represented fairly, and the discussion respects what existing evidence actually shows.

D3 concerns whether the paper earns its conclusions through a consistent chain of reasoning. Editorial oversight requires the argument reviewers to examine whether premises, analyses, and conclusions agree and whether any inferential gap damages the principal message.

D4 concerns the paper's usefulness to readers working beyond its immediate specialty. From an editorial perspective, specialist concepts need sufficient explanation, and any claimed value across research areas needs support that readers from those areas can evaluate.

D5 concerns how effectively the presentation enables readers to follow and assess the work. The editorial assessment covers the sequence of sections, precision of the prose, intelligibility of visual and tabular material, and presentation requirements where an authorized basis for checking them is available.

D6 concerns whether the work offers a meaningful new scholarly contribution for its intended audience. The editor must distinguish an established contribution from a merely asserted one and assess audience suitability only against available, authorized target information; no target alignment is established at this stage.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: A navigable progression from question to conclusions, clearly introduced terminology and notation, readable figures and tables with sufficient captions, and compliance with any subsequently supplied authoritative presentation requirements.
what_triggers_block: Pervasive organizational or explanatory deficiencies make the central account difficult to reconstruct and require substantial rewriting or restructuring before readers can assess it reliably.
what_triggers_warn: Isolated ambiguities, repetition, caption omissions, or presentation inconsistencies impede reading but can be corrected locally without reorganizing the central account.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: An explicit and supported account of what the work adds beyond relevant prior research, why that addition matters, and whom it serves; assess target-specific suitability only if authoritative target information becomes available.
what_triggers_block: A potentially substantive contribution cannot be assessed because its distinction from prior work or its significance is inadequately substantiated, requiring substantial clarification or additional supporting analysis within the study's existing direction.
what_triggers_warn: The contribution and its value are assessable, but localized overstatement or imprecise positioning requires narrower wording or clearer explanation of the intended audience.
what_triggers_fatal: Evidence establishes that the work supplies no original scholarly contribution, or that a subsequently documented target scope is fundamentally incompatible with the work and cannot be addressed without replacing its research objective.
