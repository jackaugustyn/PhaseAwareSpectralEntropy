## Contract Paraphrase

D1 requires a defensible connection between the research question, experimental design, observations, and statistical conclusions. From a methodological perspective, that includes appropriate experimental units, transparent data processing, uncertainty estimates suited to the dependence structure, and enough procedural detail to reproduce the analysis.

D2 requires scientific concepts, terminology, and accounts of previous research to be accurate. Methodologically, the chosen measurements and assumptions must represent the domain constructs they are intended to assess, and interpretations must respect established limitations of those constructs.

D3 requires the central reasoning to remain consistent from the stated question through the reported evidence to the conclusions. A methodological reading should distinguish direct observations from explanatory or general claims and check that each inferential step has adequate support.

D4 requires readers from neighboring disciplines to understand the definitions, assumptions, and significance of the work. From a methodological perspective, claims that a procedure or finding transfers across fields need explicit justification and clearly stated conditions of use.

D5 requires an organization and presentation that let readers follow and assess the work, including its visual evidence and required publication format. Methodologically useful exposition makes procedures, comparison conditions, and reported quantities traceable without forcing readers to reconstruct essential analytical steps.

D6 concerns whether the contribution falls within the intended publication's remit and offers sufficient originality and significance for its audience. The methodological connection is whether the evidence can substantiate the stated advance and distinguish it from existing capabilities; this paraphrase makes no assessment of venue alignment.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor
dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Assess the match between research questions and experimental design; experimental units, sampling and independence; controls and comparison fairness; preprocessing and parameter choices; effect sizes, uncertainty, multiplicity and robustness where applicable; and procedural information sufficient for independent reproduction.
what_triggers_block: A consequential but remediable gap in experimental specification, control adequacy, statistical treatment, or reproducibility prevents reliable assessment of a central result and requires substantial clarification, reanalysis, or additional supporting evidence.
what_triggers_warn: A localized reporting omission or limited sensitivity check reduces precision or ease of reproduction while leaving the central design and inference assessable and credible.
what_triggers_fatal: A demonstrated foundational design or measurement defect makes the central quantity unidentifiable or the central evidence invalid, with no defensible recovery through the available observations and procedure without replacing the study's evidential basis.

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Trace each central conclusion to the observations and analysis that support it; check consistency of definitions and assumptions; distinguish descriptive, explanatory, predictive, and generalization claims; and assess whether alternative explanations and stated limitations constrain the conclusions appropriately.
what_triggers_block: A central claim exceeds the demonstrated scope or depends on an unsupported inferential step that requires substantial qualification, restructuring, or further evidence while retaining a defensible core thesis.
what_triggers_warn: An ambiguous transition, isolated overstatement, or incompletely stated limitation can be corrected locally without changing the central reasoning or its evidential support.
what_triggers_fatal: The central thesis depends on a demonstrated logical contradiction or invalid inference whose removal eliminates the claimed result, so repair would require replacing the thesis rather than clarifying or narrowing it.
