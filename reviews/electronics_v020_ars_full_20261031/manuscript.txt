Experimental Diagnostics of IV Diffusion
Paweł Augustynowicz 1,∗

                                        1   Faculty of Cybernetics, Military University of Technology, ul. gen. Sylwestra Kaliskiego 2, 00-908 Warsaw,

                                        Abstract                                                                                                          1


                                        Early nonce responses reflect the round function, output finalization, and measurement.                           2

                                        We separate these stages in reduced ChaCha20 and Salsa20 using exact algebra and paired                           3

                                        experiments on independent key cohorts. Both two-round cores exhibit recurring 512-bit                            4

                                        structure; within-block rotations remove approximately 96% and 94% of their nonzero-                              5

                                        frequency entropy contrasts. An independent scan confirms a Salsa20/2 nonce-word                                  6

                                        entropy gap of 0.79059 bits. This asymmetry precedes feed-forward, while modular addi-                            7

                                        tion increases it and introduces a predicted mixed nonce interaction. A conditional identity                      8

                                        bounds the mean singleton response of Salsa20/2 output word 7 to flips in the second nonce                        9

                                        word by 31/32 changed bits, with the remaining state fixed, and gives its exact local rank.                      10

                                        A third-round update ends the sampled conditional affinity of the unfinalized word-7 map                         11

                                        while substantial block-scale structure persists in standard output. Two frozen empirical                        12

                                        models predict entropy-gap signs on new keys; retaining conditional positional profiles                          13

                                        improves mean nonzero-frequency spectra at all tested resolutions, but not every entropy                         14

                                        gap. Spectral entropy thus interprets construction-linked responses without establishing                         15

                                        superior detector sensitivity, an attack, or full-round insecurity.                                              16

                                        avalanche effect; spectral diagnostics; feed-forward                                                             18

                                     3. Materials and Methods                                                                                53

                                     3.1. Common Inputs, References, and Independent Cohorts                                                 54

                                           Each principal cohort has 80 matched pairs of separately derived 256-bit cipher keys              55

                                     and four shared 64-bit base nonces v per pair. Salsa20 uses v; ChaCha20 uses 032 ∥v; counters           56

                                     start at zero. BLAKE2b domains separate keys and control secrets [32]; SHAKE256 derives                 57

                                     nonces and reference streams [33]. Each key/cipher/configuration reuses one reference                   58

                                     stream per nonce, with collision-checked disjoint base/variant groups. Under an ideal                   59

                                     random-function model, distinct variants give independent uniform differences despite                   60

                                     sharing a base. SHAKE256 realizes the reuse pattern computationally, without proving                    61

                                     ideal randomness.                                                                                       62

                                           Table 1 separates the cohorts. Alignment uses singleton masks at bytewise most-                   63

                                     significant-bit (MSB) first positions 0, 21, 42, 63; other cohorts scan all 64 singletons with          64

                                     paired nonce-word offsets. State-word bits are least-significant-bit (LSB) first. Protocols             65

                                     preceded generation; independent cohorts mean new keys, not another team. Standard                      66

                                     outputs passed vectors or independent library checks; reduced and odd-round cores were                  67

                                     checked against independent scalar rounds.                                                              68

                                     4.1. Round Function: Recurring Structure and Conditional Rank                                                                          156

                                          Both two-round cores show recurring 512-bit structure (Figure 1a). Their four-position                                            157

                                     range exceeds the reference by 17.87 bits [17.53, 18.21] for ChaCha20 and 30.41 [30.03,                                                158

                                     30.78] for Salsa20. Their mean HW deficits are 21.77 and 37.57 bits per 128-bit segment,                                               159

                                     respectively. The independent 64-mask scan confirms a Salsa20 nonce-word entropy gap                                                   160

                                     of +0.79059 bits (Table 2); every key-level and every offset-averaged gap is positive. The                                             161

                                     descriptive ChaCha20 gap is −0.00036 bits [−0.00393, 0.00307].                                                                         162

                                     5. Scope and Discussion                                                                              258

                                           The direct benchmark detects the same large two-round departures: mean HW,                     259

                                     avalanche RMS, four-position range, lag-four correlation, and both entropy summaries                 260

                                     have pointwise intervals excluding zero. Their four- and 20-round intervals include zero.            261

                                     Thus spectral decomposition explains aspects of an already detectable response; no cali-             262

                                     brated detector-power advantage was established. Small higher-round contrasts, including             263

                                     occasional nonzero pointwise effects in other cohorts, establish neither simultaneous equiv-         264

                                     alence nor security.                                                                                 265

                                           Ancillary studies delimit the diagnostic. The initial seven-cipher comparison (Grain-          266

                                     128, Trivium, MICKEY 2.0, HC-128, Rabbit, ChaCha20, Salsa20; 80 keys each) used four                 267

                                     bases, four distributed singleton masks and 4096-bit windows (Q = 16, s = 128). All                  268

                                     standard-setting mean HMP contrasts were below 0.001 bits in magnitude with pointwise                269

                                     intervals covering zero. Grain-128/0 and Trivium/64 instead showed transient contrasts               270

                                     nearly absent from the last 16 segments; initialization counts are not comparable to ARX             271

                                     rounds [5,37,38]. On 80 fresh Grain keys and four bases, the zero-clock bit-64-minus-bit-            272

                                     0 gap replicated at +0.09526 bits [0.08921, 0.10126], p = 10−5 , and became small after              273

                                     standard initialization.                                                                             274

                                           An 80-key Trivium pilot used degree-d derivatives (d = 2, 4, 6) over 512 bits: the first d     275

                                     LSB-first positions of {0, 3, . . . , 69}, with bases zero outside the cube or setting one of bits   276

                                     72, 75, 78. Deficits survived 12-cell max-|t| correction at 192/384 clocks but not 768/1152;         277

                                     the published attack’s key conditions were not imposed [39].                                         278

                                           A5/1 used 80 keys, four frames, 22 singleton masks and 114 output bits. The 20-clock           279

                                     flip-fraction test was unresolved (p = 0.719). At 100 standard clocks, none of 44 mask-              280

                                     specific flip-fraction/entropy tests (19 six-bit HW samples) survived correction (minimum            281

                                     p = 0.669). Another 80 keys confirmed the zero-clock, LSB-first bit-21-minus-bit-0 gradient:         282

                                     −0.04350 [−0.05433, −0.03281], p = 10−5 . Grain/A5 validations used 100,000 sign flips               283

                                     and 10,000 bootstrap resamples; Trivium/A5 searches used 50,000 whole-key max-|t| sign               284

                                     flips. A quiet standard diagnostic thus coexists with A5/1 state-recovery weaknesses [40].           285

                                           The ARX results concern the tested masks, block-aligned widths, windows up to 8192             286

                                     bits, and selected rounds. Simulations and deterministic witnesses do not enlarge the key            287

                                     cohorts. Nonstandard finalizations isolate construction effects, while empirical models              288

                                     summarize selected observables. These measurements suggest positions and differences                 289

                                     for further analysis but provide no key hypothesis, attack complexity, or attack advantage.          290



                                     6. Conclusions                                                                                       291

                                          Exact identities explain a weak Salsa20/2 output-word response and show why equal               292

                                     means can conceal different conditional ranks. Paired finalizations locate the nonce-word            293

                                     asymmetry before feed-forward and isolate an interaction introduced by modular addition.             294

                                     Rotation, resolution, and frozen prediction controls connect these construction effects to           295

                                     spectral summaries. The third-round experiment shows that conditional affinity of the                296

                                     unfinalized word-7 map can end while block-scale structure persists in standard output.              297

                                     These results diagnose reduced-round mechanisms without establishing superior detection              298

                                     or full-round vulnerability.                                                                         299
