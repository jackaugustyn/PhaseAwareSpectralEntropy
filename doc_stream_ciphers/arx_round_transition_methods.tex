\subsection{A Single-Round Transition and an Operation Checkpoint}\label{sec:roundtransitionmethods}

A prospective follow-up uses 80 new matched key pairs, four shared 64-bit base nonces, the 64 singleton masks and 38 derivative pairs of Section~\ref{sec:ffmixed}, and 8192 output bits at two, three, and four \emph{single} rounds. Round 3 is a column round in both cores; the odd-round variants are experimental constructions. A new vectorized evaluator is checked against independently coded scalar rounds and the existing even-round implementations. Finalizations share working states; reference streams have separate round domains and are coupled across finalizations within each round.

Let $A^{\rm within}_r$ be unfinalized Salsa20 output-word-7 derivative HW averaged over the two fixed within-$x_7$ pairs $(0,15)$ and $(7,30)$, four bases, and 16 blocks. The first planned endpoint is $T_1=\Delta A^{\rm within}_3-\Delta A^{\rm within}_2$, after cipher-minus-reference subtraction. The second is $T_2=\Delta F_{512,3}-\Delta F_{512,2}$ for Salsa20 with additive finalization and 128-bit segments (Equation~\eqref{eq:blockpower}), pooling four powers per singleton mask before averaging the reference-adjusted fractions across all 64 masks. No spectrum is computed from second-order derivatives. Both endpoints use 100,000 two-sided whole-key sign flips, plus-one $p$ values, joint Holm correction, and 5000 paired whole-key bootstrap resamples. Other comparisons, including changes in mixed interaction $J$, are descriptive.

Stored uint32 derivatives locate the response immediately before and after the $c=w_7$ update in quarter round $(15,3,7,11)$ of round 3 (a column round). These checkpoints equal $W_2[7]$ and $W_3[7]$, respectively, because no other update in that round writes word 7. We check those equalities exactly. Two-round zeros follow algebraically; a nonzero third-round witness refutes affinity for that sampled setting. Checkpoint counts are not independent statistical replicates.
